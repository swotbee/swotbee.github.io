1. CUSTOMER
[ ] Company and relevant buyer/contact associations are correct.
[ ] Buyer, payer and signing roles are distinguished where needed.
[ ] Required custom-object associations are checked where applicable.

2. PRODUCTS AND SCOPE
[ ] All intended products/services are included without unwanted duplicates.
[ ] Quantities and units match the intended offer.
[ ] Placeholder or obsolete products are resolved.

3. PRICING AND TIMING
[ ] Currency, unit prices and discounts match the intended offer.
[ ] Discount or other commercial exceptions have an authorized decision.
[ ] One-time vs recurring charges, frequency, start dates and terms are correct.
[ ] Phased prices and differences from the deal amount are explained.

4. PROPOSAL RELEASE
[ ] Rep, reviewer and quote creator/sender have clear responsibilities.
[ ] Generated proposal matches the reviewed customer and commercial data.
[ ] Review decision, time and version are recorded.
[ ] Open corrections have owners; unresolved items are not silently accepted.

5. CHANGE AND EXCEPTION CONTROL
[ ] Changes invalidating the decision are defined and assigned to an owner.
[ ] Product/quantity/price/discount/currency/term/customer changes receive recheck.
[ ] Exception reason, decision-maker and scope are recorded.
[ ] Prior review evidence is retained when a new version is reviewed.
