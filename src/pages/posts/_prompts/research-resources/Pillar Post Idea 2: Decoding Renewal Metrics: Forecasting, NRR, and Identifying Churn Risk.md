
**Pillar Post Idea 2: Decoding Renewal Metrics: Forecasting, NRR, and Identifying Churn Risk**

**Core Premise:** This pillar will focus entirely on the data, dashboarding, and unit economics that drive the renewal pipeline. It will help RevOps leaders cut through vanity metrics and establish a single source of truth for measuring renewal rates, forecasting ARR, and catching churn signals before they impact the bottom line.

7. **Using Product-Qualified Signals to Forecast Expansion:** How tracking specific usage quotas and feature adoption can alert your team to upsell opportunities right before a renewal,,.
