# Pillar + Cluster Content Plan

Strategy: one honest, current, source-cited pillar that owns the high-intent scalability terms plus the wide-open freshness gaps, surrounded by cluster posts that each target a specific keyword group and link up to the pillar. The wedge is radical honesty plus freshness: real pricing, real cons, a real decision framework, and 2026 naming, against a field that is either stale (2018 Growth Suite) or evasive (no numbers).

## The cluster posts

Each links up to the pillar and targets one keyword group or unmet question. Ordered by build priority (high gap plus low difficulty first).

1. HubSpot Growth Suite Is Now the Customer Platform: What Changed and What It Costs in 2026
   - Keywords: hubspot growth suite (70), growth suite onboarding (40), hubspot growth suite onboarding (40), hubspot growth suite pricing, growth stack hubspot.
   - Why: the entire SERP is dated 2018 content using a dead product name. Freshness play, near-zero real competition. Corrects the naming, maps old to new, gives current pricing.

2. Does HubSpot Scale With Your Business? An Honest Cost-and-Limits Breakdown
   - Keywords: does hubspot crm scale with business growth (110), hubspot crm scalability with business growth (110), hubspot crm scalability growth enterprise.
   - Why: the commercial core. Deep-dive companion to the pillar's scaling section: the real numbers, the tier cliffs, the 300 to 400 user ceiling, when to stay vs migrate.

3. HubSpot Pricing at Scale: The Bill Nobody Warns You About (with a TCO model)
   - Keywords: hubspot growth suite pricing, sales hub pricing scale with team growth, marketing hub pricing scale contact list.
   - Why: the highest-value content gap. Stage-by-stage TCO (seed to Series B), contact-tier hygiene, renewal negotiation. Strong lead-gen / consulting hook for SwotBee.

4. Loop Marketing vs Product-Led Growth: How HubSpot's New Framework Actually Fits
   - Keywords: hubspot loop marketing vs growth marketing vs product-led growth b2b, loop marketing hubspot vs product-led growth b2b, hubspot product led growth.
   - Why: nobody connects Loop Marketing to PLG. Unclaimed keyword bridge, fast win. Operationalizes the genuinely new part (answer-engine optimization / LLM discoverability).

5. The HubSpot Growth Frameworks Explained: Funnel, Flywheel, Loop Marketing, and Four Fits
   - Keywords: hubspot growth framework, hubspot growth strategy, growth marketing hubspot, brian balfour hubspot growth framework, hubspot conversational growth strategy.
   - Why: maps the four overlapping models on one diagram with "use this when" guidance. Clarity nobody else provides; ties rigorous growth theory (Four Fits) to HubSpot's brand language.

6. Growth-Driven Design on HubSpot in 2026: Is It Still Worth It?
   - Keywords: growth driven design hubspot (70, KD 8), hubspot growth driven design, what is growth driven design hubspot, three stages of growth driven design, hubspot growth driven design certification.
   - Why: official cert is retired but pages still rank as if live; top results are "answers" dumps. Accurate, honest "when is GDD overkill / real ROI" piece corrects the record. (Note: we will NOT publish cert cheat answers; we provide a legitimate study-guide structure.)

7. HubSpot Revenue-Growth Case Studies: Real Numbers in One Table
