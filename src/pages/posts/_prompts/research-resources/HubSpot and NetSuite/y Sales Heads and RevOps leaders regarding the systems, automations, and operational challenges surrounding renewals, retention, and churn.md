Here are the top questions asked by Sales Heads and RevOps leaders regarding the systems, automations, and operational challenges surrounding renewals, retention, and churn. These questions completely exclude software pricing and licensing discussions:

**Subscription Management & CPQ Complexity**
1. **"How do you automate contract upgrades, downgrades, and co-terming when dealing with thousands of active SaaS subscriptions and multiple pricing models?"**
2. **"Is it better to handle recurring revenue, subscription management, and CPQ natively inside the CRM, or should it be moved entirely to an external billing/ERP system?"**
3. **"How do you operationally handle legacy pricing structures, grandfathered contracts, and potential limited-time offers during the renewal quoting process as the company scales?"**
4. **"How are you tracking and communicating renewals, expansions, and terminations seamlessly across disparate CRM and accounting systems without creating data silos?"**

**Automating Renewal Workflows & Communications**
5. **"What is the best way to automate upcoming subscription renewal communications based on contract data without it feeling robotic or impersonal to the customer?"**
6. **"How do you effectively automate your customer retention flow to resurrect churning customers in a non-pushy way?"**
7. **"How do we orchestrate a unified customer journey so that Customer Success can seamlessly take over the account after Sales closes, leading to a frictionless renewal?"**
8. **"How can we use hyper-personalized automation (like video or dynamic content) at scale to retain customers and reduce churn during the renewal window?"**
9. **"How do you optimize your offboarding flow to collect honest feedback from churning customers, and can that process be productized to save the renewal before they leave?"**

**Identifying Churn Risk & Health Scoring**
10. **"Has anyone successfully used predictive modeling or AI anomaly detection to forecast renewal rates and flag early churn risks?"**
11. **"What are the most crucial customer health metrics we should be monitoring in our CRM to flag at-risk accounts well before the renewal discussion happens?"**
12. **"Should we be using a traditional 'churn risk score', or is it better to pivot our focus to 'conversion/expansion prediction' instead?"**
13. **"How do you set up a monthly churn insights review that actually drives proactive retention strategies rather than just looking at lagging revenue metrics?"**
14. **"Why is churn considered a 'silent killer', and how can we systematically identify and measure usage drop-offs before they impact our top-line renewal numbers?"**

**Team Alignment, Expansion & Customer Success**
15. **"How do we give sales reps real-time visibility into open support tickets and service histories so they aren't blindsided by complaints during a renewal negotiation?"**
16. **"How do we transition our Customer Success mindset from a 'cost center' into a 'profit/growth center' focused heavily on driving expansion revenue at renewal?"**
17. **"Why is retention considered the #1 weapon to drive expansion revenue, and what is the best operational process to track it?"**
18. **"How can customer support conversations and help desk data be systematically leveraged to identify expansion or upsell opportunities prior to the renewal date?"**
19. **"How do you orchestrate cross-functional alignment so that marketing, sales, and product teams are all working toward the same 'North Star' retention metric?"**
20. **"What specific processes, automations, and partnerships are required to prevent churn and handle renewals for complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise customers?"**

**Onboarding as a Retention Driver**
21. **"How do you strategically plan a user onboarding revamp to establish product habits early, ensuring higher engagement when renewal time comes around?"**
22. **"Faster isn't always better: Has anyone improved long-term retention and renewal rates by actually adding friction to their user onboarding process to ensure proper setup?"**
