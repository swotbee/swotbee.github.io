# Template Prompt for Simple Isometric Visualizations

Use this template whenever you need a clear, eye-catching LinkedIn post graphic with up to five key symbols. Simply swap in your concept, labels, and colors:

**“Isometric [Concept Name] Visualization
Split-screen isometric scene illustrating [left narrative] vs. [right narrative].
Caption (bottom-center): “[Main Title]” in a clean, modern sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat, Open Sans).
Left panel: A [primary object] in [left base color] (\#hex) on [surface/shape], labeled “[Left Label]” in dark gray (\#2E3A46). No additional elements—symbolizing [left metaphor].
Right panel: The same [primary object] now [transformed action] with an accent element in [accent color] (\#hex), labeled “[Right Label]” in white or dark gray. Include up to two subtle symbols (e.g., arrows, gust lines, glow) in a lighter shade of the accent color to show [right metaphor].
Background: Soft cream (\#fef4ea) with a light-blue gradient sky (\#c8d7e4). Apply rounded corners and a gentle drop shadow to panels for depth.
Key Metaphors:

- [Symbol 1] = [Meaning]
- [Symbol 2] = [Meaning]
- [Symbol 3] = [Meaning]

Style notes:

- Max 4–5 objects/symbols.
- Flat isometric shapes with minimal line-weights.
- Consistent color palette: 2 neutrals, 1 base hue, 1 accent hue.
- Legible labels in uppercase, 14–16 pt size.
- Soft shadows and subtle gradients for dimension.

---

Example usage:

“Isometric Adoption vs. Attrition Visualization
Split-screen isometric scene illustrating early adopters vs. churn.
Caption (bottom-center): “User Lifecycle” in Montserrat.
Left panel: A teal cube (\#3BAAA8) labeled “Onboarding” on a glassy platform, no motion—symbolizing stagnant engagement.
Right panel: The same cube now with a billowing yellow ribbon (\#F4C150) and two arrow symbols in light yellow (\#F9DC8C), labeled “Retention,” moving toward a glowing star (\#F4C150)—representing sustained loyalty.
…”

Simply adjust the objects, labels, and hex colors to fit your topic.
