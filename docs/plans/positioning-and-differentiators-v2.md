## Case Studies (Draft Outlines)

### Case Study 1: Energy/Utilities — Unified Revenue Operations
**Challenge:** Multiple sales divisions (inside sales, equipment, multi-fuel) operating in separate pipelines with no cross-visibility. ERP integration needed for meter readings and installation alerts. Renewals with multi-line items were manual and error-prone. Custom quoting was slow and inconsistent.

**What we did:**
- Unified sales pipelines across all divisions
- Integrated ERP for meter readings, installation alerts, and customer onboarding support
- Built automated renewal workflows with multi-line item support that reads usage data to recommend renewal plans
- Created custom quoting templates that reduced quote creation effort by 90%

**Results:**
- Renewal accuracy up ~50%
- Quote creation time reduced by 90%
- Sales team shifted from admin work to growth activities

---

### Case Study 2: Manufacturing — Post-Sales Automation
**Challenge:** No automated handoff from sales to production. Service requests were manually routed. Shipping and production status updates never reached the sales team. Quote follow-ups were inconsistent.

**What we did:**
- Implemented quote follow-ups, production status alerts, and shipping notifications with tracking
- Built service handoffs using custom objects ("Freight Required", "Production Status")
- Connected Service Hub ticketing to IoT data for real-time equipment status
- Automated lifecycle stages to prevent manual overrides for audit trails

**Results:**
- 30% faster response times
- 25% quicker resolution via Service Hub ticketing
- Full production-to-sales visibility

---

### Case Study 3: Compliance & Regulated Industries
**Challenge:** Companies in regulated sectors need audit trails, environmental tracking, and data handling controls that HubSpot doesn't provide out of the box.

**What we did:**
- Enforced automated lifecycle stages preventing manual overrides (audit trail integrity)
- Integrated ISO 14001-like environmental tracking for utilities/energy clients
- Built HIPAA/GDPR controls with encrypted PHI handling
- Created compliance dashboards for regulatory reporting

**Results:**
- Full audit trail compliance
- Automated regulatory reporting
- Zero manual override violations
