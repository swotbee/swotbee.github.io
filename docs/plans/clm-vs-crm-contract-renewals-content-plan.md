## Differentiation

Most ranking pages compare feature lists. This article focuses on the operating boundary during renewals: which system owns the document, which owns the revenue event, which owns billing truth, and which fields must cross each handoff.

The core principle is that system ownership is assigned field by field. CLM owns negotiated language and executed contract evidence. CRM owns customer work, renewal opportunities and forecasts. ERP or billing owns invoices and payment. A product suite may combine these capabilities, but the logical ownership rules still apply.
