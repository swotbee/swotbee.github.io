## Content Tone Guidelines

Based on the feedback and positioning:

1. **Empathy first, credentials second** — "We know your pipeline is unreliable" before "We're HubSpot Solutions Partners"
2. **Specific, not vague** — "27-rule deal quality framework" not "comprehensive quality approach"
3. **Industry-aware** — mention Energy, Manufacturing, SaaS specifically, not "various industries"
4. **Subtle on proprietary tools** — mention capabilities ("automated renewal tracking") not product names ("Smart Deal Inspector") on service pages
5. **Action-oriented** — "Leads flowing, deals closing faster from day one" not "we enable business transformation"
6. **No jargon** — plain language, short sentences, scannable
