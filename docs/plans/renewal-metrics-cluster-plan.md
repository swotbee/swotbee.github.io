renewal pillar focused on metrics, dashboards, and unit economics. Source brief:

## How this differs from Pillar 1
- **Pillar 1** (`hubspot-renewal-pipeline-complete-guide`): how to BUILD and run a renewal
  pipeline (stages, properties, workflows, reminders, quoting, ownership).
- **Pillar 2** (this plan): how to MEASURE renewals. Forecasting, retention metrics (NRR/GRR),
  churn-risk detection, and the unit economics RevOps leaders report to the board and investors.

The two pillars cross-link but target different intent (build vs measure).

- core_topic: SaaS / B2B renewal metrics, forecasting, and churn-risk analytics (HubSpot-centric where natural).
- audience_persona: RevOps leaders, VP Ops/Finance, CS leaders at mid-market (swotbee ICP).
- authority_goal: own "how to measure and forecast renewals" the way Pillar 1 owns "how to run them."
- differentiation_strategy: cut through vanity metrics; cohort truth over blended averages;
